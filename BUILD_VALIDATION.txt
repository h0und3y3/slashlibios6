Renderer limitation:
iOS 6 ImageIO has no public region decoder. Tall pages are first downsampled to a
maximum longest edge of 3072 px (energy saving) or 4096 px (normal), then written
as 768-pixel JPEG strips. This bounds memory and allows disk-backed strip loading,
but is not native-resolution tile decoding.
